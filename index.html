01 who we are

Datum provides the infrastructure to build and deliver sophisticated interactive 3D representations of physical and virtual spaces.

We redefine how governments and enterprises engage with space, culture, and data—unlocking the full potential of their spatial intelligence through storytelling, analytics, and AI.

Datum facilitates the digital transformation of physical assets through immersive 3D experiences. We believe that a space's value is unlocked only when data is paired with context and expert guidance. Datum enables governments and industries to publish intelligent, interactive representations of their environments, ensuring seamless information transfer across all organizational levels.

Datum is a way for creating and delivering interactive 3D representations of any spaces.

By combining scene management with AI-guided storytelling, we enable governments and enterprises to publish high-fidelity, interactive experiences that scale across web, mobile, and other platforms.

02 what we do

Our Technologies

Photorealistic 3D Scenes

We use modern 3D technology for scene representations to capture any spaces with high visual fidelity while keeping them lightweight, fast, and scalable across platforms.

  • Near photoreal visuals
  • Optimized for web, mobile, and other platforms
  • Scales to huge environments

Rendering & Runtime Engine

At the core of Datum is a custom rendering and runtime engine built specifically for smooth, interactive 3D experiences in any applications.

  • LOD & streaming perfomance
  • Smooth real time rendering
  • Full control of UX and input

SDK & Platform Integration

The Datum SDK can be used on its own or together with Datum Studio. When paired, scenes, storytelling, and AI behavior configured in Studio are delivered through the SDK with minimal integration effort.

  • Lightweight SDK integration
  • Runs on web, mobile, and other platforms
  • Control scene logic and AI

AI-guided Interaction Layer

An AI interaction layer turns 3D scenes into guided, explainable experiences by combining spatial context with conversational interaction, scalable to large environments.

  • Scene-aware AI
  • Configurable tone and behavior
  • Natural nav and discovery

05 Storytelling

Discover articles, interviews, and experiments exploring how technology reshapes narrative and perception. We turn research into prototypes — from Gaussian splats to interactive data stories.

Méridien explores cities, places, and cultural landmarks around the globe. Travelling across meridians and parallels, we search for breakthroughs in engineering, architecture, culture, and technology, always asking: how is this possible, why is it happening, and what does it mean?

We explore immersive media formats and experiment with new ways of presenting data and stories. Méridien is the laboratory for storytelling, where we research, prototype, and reimagine how history, culture, and cities can be experienced. From Gaussian splatting to interactive maps, we test emerging tools to push narrative boundaries.

Our goal is simple: to reinvent how you see cities, history, and culture.

The result is slow, thoughtful, and visually rich journalism. We merge digital reality and data analysis to reveal the world — past and present — with depth and accuracy. Méridien is for people who are curious about the world and eager to explore it in new ways.
