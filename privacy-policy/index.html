Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how The Datum collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

Your information is used to provide and improve our services, respond to inquiries, process requests for demos, and communicate updates. We may also use aggregated data for analytics and service enhancements.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience, analyze traffic, and personalize content. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

The Datum reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@thedatum.ai
